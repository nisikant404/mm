## Phase Details

**Phase 1: AI Chatbot Core**
Goal: Build the conversational AI symptom checker to capture symptoms and diagnose issues.
Requirements: CHK-01, CHK-02
Success criteria:
1. User can type symptoms into a chat interface.
2. AI responds dynamically and outputs a recognized probable diagnosis and medical domain.

**Phase 2: Dynamic Doctor List & Booking**
Goal: Render a side-list of matching doctors with experience stats and functional booking buttons.
Requirements: DOC-01, DOC-02, APT-01
Success criteria:
1. Matching doctors render dynamically based on the AI's determined domain.
2. Doctor experience is clearly visible.
3. Clicking "Book" raises a valid appointment ticket for the patient.
